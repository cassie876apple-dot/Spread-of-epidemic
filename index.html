SIR Model Explanation

This simulation is based on the classical SIR epidemiological model, dividing a closed population into Susceptible (S), Infected (I), and Recovered (R).

Equations: 

new infections = β × S × I / N
new recoveries = γ × I

S(t+1) = S(t) − new infections
I(t+1) = I(t) + new infections − new recoveries
R(t+1) = R(t) + new recoveries

β: infection rate, γ: recovery rate, N: total population.

Visualized with moving emojis to make math intuitive.

Limitations

Homogeneous mixing, no age or spatial factors, no births/deaths, no interventions.

Epidemic Spread
Epidemic spread describes how infectious diseases propagate through a population. Factors: contact, proximity, probability, mobility, social behavior, environment.
Model Types
SI/SIR/SEIR/Agent-based models each balance realism & simplicity. Agent-based simulates individuals with unique behaviors.
Why SIR?
SIR captures essential epidemic behavior simply, mathematically clear, and computationally efficient.
Future Improvements
Add vaccination, immunity loss, age-stratification, network-based contact to reflect real-world epidemics more accurately.
