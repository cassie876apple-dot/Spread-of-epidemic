Model Explanation

This project uses the SIR epidemiological model to simulate how an infectious disease spreads through a closed population.

Individuals are divided into three groups:

  • Susceptible (S): healthy individuals who can become infected
  • Infected (I): individuals who are currently infected and can spread the disease
  • Recovered (R): individuals who have recovered and are no longer infectious

The simulation updates these groups over time using infection rate (β) and recovery rate (γ). Each moving emoji represents a person, making the abstract math model more intuitive and visual.

Limitations

This model assumes a closed population, random movement, and equal contact probability. Real-world factors such as age, immunity differences, social networks, and interventions are not included.
